dither — IPC graph

Companion to ipc-landscape.html. Same primitives and processes — but laid out as a directed graph: who writes to what, who reads from what, who spawns what. Solid lines = writes / in-process calls. Dashed lines = reads. Arrowhead always points at the target. Each line is colored by the primitive it touches.

How to read it. Boxes are the entities — processes at top (CLI · daemon · plugin), filesystem primitives at bottom (each in its own color). Lines are interactions. Follow any colored line from its source to its arrowhead to see one piece of the system. Solid = write or in-process call. Dashed = read.
The funnel. Scheduler, Watcher, Refirer all funnel into fireWithSuppress. Anything that ever runs a plugin goes through that one hub — which is also where LoopDetector halts chains that hit depth ≥ 3.
The split brain. The daemon has two engines: the fire path (Scheduler/Watcher/Refirer → fireWithSuppress → runPlugin → spawn Deno) for plugin work, and the Reconciler (1 Hz, marker-driven) for qmd index/embed jobs. They share only the run-log and the lock primitives.
The no-daemon paths. dither search, dither get, dither status read sqlite / status.json directly in the CLI process — those flows never touch the daemon (only dashed gray lines from CLI to qmd-index.sqlite and status.json).
